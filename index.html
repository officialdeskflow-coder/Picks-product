Make sure it’s genuine

Fake and mislabelled supplements are common. Three checks every time:

  1. Check the seller

    On Amazon or Flipkart, the seller should be the brand itself or its official store. Skip unknown sellers with lower prices.

  2. Check the seal

    When it arrives, the inner foil seal should be intact. A broken seal or odd smell means return it.

  3. Check the code

    Most big brands print a scratch or QR authenticity code. Verify it on the brand’s website before your first scoop.

    Supplements fill gaps in your diet. They don’t replace meals, sleep or training.

    If you have a medical condition or take medicine, check with your doctor before starting.
